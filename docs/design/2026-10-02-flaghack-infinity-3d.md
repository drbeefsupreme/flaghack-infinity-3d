# Flaghack Infinity 3D

A fast, 3D, single-player 1v1v1v1 vexillomantic action-strategy game in the browser
(three.js + TypeScript, `web/`). You are a vexillomancer at a burn. You throw, plant,
pull and steal yellow Survey Flags on an invisible aperiodic **Ley Lattice**, build
Fortnite-speed structures, command hippies from the **Geomantic Command Center**, and
conquer rival camps by enclosing their Flag Hearth inside your Survey until it is
**overwritten**. The last vexillomancer with a Hearth wins.

The requirements doc (origin) still governs the product: command-heavy (≈75% camp,
hippie and Survey command, ≈25% direct intervention), Flags as reality operators,
NPCs obey the same visible systems, drugs/buildings/chakras all change decisions.

## 1. Pillars

1. **Flags move reality.** Every territory change comes from Flags at Ley Nodes. Walls,
   buildings and units never enclose territory; they protect, carry and contest Flags.
2. **Speed.** Planting is a tap, throwing is a flick, walls pop instantly. A camp can be
   enclosed in under a minute of focused play. Anything slow (capture, rituals) is
   telegraphed and contestable.
3. **The Crystal is real physics.** The Ley Lattice is a genuine Penrose rhombus tiling
   projected from a 5-dimensional lattice. Its quasicrystal behaviour (phason flips,
   observation, superposition, 5-fold focus points, self-similar implication) is the
   magic system.
4. **Readable chaos.** Spectacle (particles, beams, shimmer) never hides state: Hearth
   stage, who encloses what, and where the next threat is are always legible.

## 2. World

- Map: 300 m × 300 m, x/z ∈ [-150, 150], flat ground at y = 0 (height comes from built
  structures and buildings). A grassy Georgia burn (Alchemy): dirt roads, tents,
  geodesic domes, art installations, porta-potty rows, tree clusters, a pond, sound camps.
- Four corner camps at (±90, ±90): faction 0 (player) at (-90, 90); see `CAMP_CENTERS`
  in `web/src/sim/constants.ts`.
- **Conquest corridor.** No Ley Node within `CAMP_CLEAR_RADIUS` (46.7 m) of a camp centre
  is ever blocked by an obstacle. Two blocked nodes that share a rhombus form a link no
  loop can cross, so blocked-ground chains could otherwise seal a Hearth forever. The
  radius is the rival loop radius (24 m) + the longest rhombus diagonal (15.2 m) + the
  worst Hearth offset (7.5 m): the outline of the facet patch around the home area is
  then always a plantable loop, on any rhombus tiling and after any phason flips.
  `web/src/sim/map/corridor.test.ts` asserts this over 24 seeds.
- Center: **The Flag**, a 26 m wooden effigy flag on the **Omega Node** (a guaranteed
  5-fold focus point). It burns at **The Burn** (sudden death, see §9).
- Lumber piles (pallets, MOOP heaps) scattered across the map; they deplete and respawn.
- Sound camps distract low-attention hippies.

## 3. The Ley Lattice (quasicrystal core)

Generated by de Bruijn's **pentagrid** method: five families of parallel lines with
directions `e_j = (cos 2πj/5, sin 2πj/5)` and offsets `γ_j` (Σγ_j = 0). Every
intersection of two grid lines becomes one rhombus. Every vertex carries an integer
5-vector `k ∈ Z⁵` (its **5D Ley coordinate**), parallel position `Σ k_j e_j · s` and
perpendicular position `Σ k_j e⊥_j` with `e⊥_j = (cos 4πj/5, sin 4πj/5)`.

- Edge length `s = LEY_EDGE = 8 m`. Rhombi: **thick** (72°/108°, "Sun facet") and
  **thin** (36°/144°, "Moon facet"). Ratio thick:thin → φ.
- Player terms: Ley Node (vertex), Ley Line (edge both of whose nodes hold same-faction
  Flags), Ley Facet (rhombus), Survey (a faction's enclosed facets).
- Nodes inside static obstacles are `blocked` (no planting).
- The tiling is translated so a 5-fold **star vertex** (five thick rhombi meeting at
  their 72° corners) sits at the map center: the **Omega Node**.

### 3.1 Survey rules (deterministic, recomputed when dirty)

1. **Flags occupy nodes.** One planted Flag per node. Flags are physical, always yellow;
   the planter's faction colour shows as a ribbon at the finial and in ley light.
2. **Implied Flags (the implied Flag fractal).** If two nodes held by faction F (real or
   implied) have an *exact* midpoint at another free, unblocked node, that node holds an
   implied Flag of F with order = max(parent orders) + 1. Iterate to order 3. Parent pairs
   reach up to 2φ·edge (`IMPLIED_REACH`), so midpoints come from straight runs at two
   scales: two collinear edges, and two collinear φ·edge steps along thick-rhombus long
   diagonals (the inflated tiling). That second scale is what makes the fractal cascade;
   at scale 1 alone no implied Flag ever has an implied child. Implied Flags count for Ley
   Lines, facets, pentacles and enclosure, cannot be pulled, and vanish when a parent
   goes. A real Flag (any faction) on the node overrides; two factions implying one node
   cancel (interference).
3. **Ley Lines.** An edge whose two nodes are both held by F (real or implied) is F's Ley
   Line.
4. **Crystallized facets.** A facet whose four nodes are all held by F.
5. **Enclosure.** Facets of F's Survey = facets that cannot reach the map boundary via
   facet adjacency without crossing one of F's Ley Lines (flood fill from boundary
   facets). Crystallized facets are automatically enclosed.
6. **Overlap / interference.** A facet in ≥ 2 factions' Surveys accumulates
   **instability** (0→1, +0.08/s; decays −0.15/s when no longer overlapped).
   - ≥ 0.35 shimmer and moiré. Units of the faction holding the most of an unstable
     facet's corners resonate (+20% work speed); every other faction's units there suffer
     Flag Psychosis (attention drains twice as fast).
   - ≥ 0.65 **Crystal discharge** lightning every few seconds (stun 1.2 s on units,
     25 damage to pieces/buildings).
   - ≥ 0.9 local phason flips in unobserved spots.

### 3.2 Phasons (the Crystal turns)

- A **phason flip** moves a degree-3 interior node `v` (neighbours `v+e1, v+e2, v+e3`) to
  `v' = v + e1 + e2 + e3`: the three rhombi of the hexagon re-tile. Node, edge and facet
  ids are reused in place; positions/topology change. Any Flag on `v` **decoheres**: it
  falls over as a loose Flag at its old spot, and its ley lines snap.
- **Phason Tide**: every 75 s (10 s warning: "The Crystal is turning…") ~5% of flippable
  nodes flip, weighted toward high **perpendicular strain**. Strain is the distance of
  the node's perp position outside the canonical acceptance window, so the lattice tends
  to heal back toward perfect Penrose order. A tide is a **wave**: the chosen nodes flip
  in order along a seeded direction as a front crosses the map over ~1.8 s.
- **Observation freezes the Crystal (Zeno).** A node is *observed* for faction F if any
  of the following hold:
  - it is within 12 m of F's vexillomancer;
  - it is within 30 m of F's GCC (Geomantic Advice);
  - it is within 22 m of F's Hearth;
  - it is within 26 m of an F Hearth Ward;
  - it is inside an F Stabilize Zone;
  - (Canon III) its Flag has Ley Lines to ≥ 2 other Flags ("every Flag had to be within
    line of sight of two other Flags").

  Tides skip nodes whose Flag is observed by its owner. Empty nodes flip freely.
  Completed loops are therefore tide-proof; dangling, half-built patterns are not.
  Finish your casting.
- **Phason Shift** (Field chakra) deliberately flips nodes at range and ignores Canon III
  self-observation; only Stabilize Zones block it. It is the precision counter to a loop.

### 3.3 Crystal focus points

- Every 5-fold **star vertex** is a Crystal Focus. Focus points appear and vanish as
  phasons flip ("invisible moving crystal focus points"). They are invisible unless
  revealed (Geomantic Advice radius, Luminous Dust, Command View near your GCC).
- **Pentacle**: when one faction holds all five neighbours of a focus (five Flags, the
  canonical minimum), a **Crystal** manifests on the focus (3 s growth). Effects:
  - +0.08 Ritual/s to the owner (scaled by growth);
  - +15% containment pressure within 45 m;
  - observes nodes within 10 m;
  - adds to C.M.I.

  The Crystal shatters when the pentacle breaks or the node flips.
- **C.M.I. (Crystal Manifestation Index)** = Σ over owned Crystals of (100 + 10 ×
  seconds alive / 10) plus 1 per Survey facet. It is a score shown like the 2017 game,
  not a currency.

### 3.4 Superposition: Flag Simulacra

GCC action "Flag Simulacra": spend 1 Flag to plant a superposed Flag on two target nodes
at once; it counts as real on both. When any enemy unit comes within 10 m of either node,
it **collapses** (seeded 50/50) to one node; the other vanishes with a glitch. "Proper
casting involves placing the flags far enough apart that they cannot all be seen at
once."

## 7. Geomantic Command Center (GCC)

The 2017 artefact from the wiki: a black **pentagonal cart** with yellow five-Flag
pinwheel sigils on its sides, cart wheels with Flag spokes, a rolled scroll, two wooden
poles carrying a yellow banner — "GEOMANTIC COMMAND CENTER / FLAG REPAIR – FLAG GIFTS /
GEOMANTIC ADVICE / FLAGELLIAN DIALECTICS" (back: "FLAG SIMULACRA") — and a tabletop
showing a **live miniature map** of the burn with Flags and red ley lines. Every faction
has one, parked beside its Hearth at start. It is mobile: hold E to push it (4 m/s).

- **Command Table**: interacting with your GCC dives the camera into the tabletop map,
  which becomes the Command View (§10). Tab opens Command View from anywhere.
- **Geomantic Advice** (passive): reveals lattice and focus points within 30 m and
  observes nodes (Zeno) within 30 m.
- **Flag Repair** (passive): every 3 s, re-plants one decohered/loose own Flag within
  20 m onto the nearest free node; repairs own pieces and buildings within 20 m (+15 HP/s).
- **Flag Gifts** (active, cooldown 3 s): spend 1 Flag to recruit a neutral hippie within
  15 m.
- **Flagellian Dialectics** (active, cooldown 40 s, 3 s channel at the cart): converts up
  to 3 enemy hippies within 14 m.
- **Flag Simulacra** (active, cooldown 20 s): §3.4.
- At 0 HP it collapses, drops nothing, and is rebuilt at the Hearth after 45 s.

Primary labour, the 75% layer.

| HP ("vibes") | 100 |
| Attention | 0–100 |

- **Jobs** with weights 0–4 set by the player (Camp Priorities):
  - **Survey**: fetch a Flag from Hearth stock or a nearby loose own Flag, then plant it
    on the nearest unreserved planned node.
  - **Gather**: chop a pile, haul lumber to the Hearth.
  - **Defend**: guard Hearth / respond to SOS pings; shove intruders and pull enemy
    Flags inside your Survey.
  - **Raid**: pull enemy Flags that enclose your Hearth (its critical Flags) first, then
    rival Flags squatting on your own plan nodes (these are replanted on the spot as
    yours: "pull, then plant"), then threats near the Hearth, Attack pings, and finally
    the nearest enemy Survey boundary; other stolen Flags go home to stock. If a camp has
    no raiders while its plan is blocked, one Survey/Defend hippie clears the blockers.
  - **Ritual**: drum at a Drum Circle.

  Idle hippies are distributed proportionally to weights over available work.
- **Direct orders** (Command View, selection + right click): move, pull this Flag, plant
  here, gather this pile, defend here, attack this piece/building, follow vexillomancer.
  In action mode: G rallies hippies within 25 m to follow you; H sends followers at the
  crosshair target.
- **Attention** drains 1/s while working (2/s in instability); at 0 the hippie gets
  distracted and wanders to the nearest sound camp for 10 s, recovering to 60. It
  recovers +4/s near own Hearth or Drum Circle when idle.
- **D.E.G.E.N. Beacons** (Distributed Entity Geolocation and Entity Navigator): every
  hippie carries one. This is how the camp knows where its hippies are and what they
  are up to.
  - Each hippie's position and current status (`HippieStatus`) is visible to its
    faction: minimap, Command View, DEGEN roster panel.
  - Pings: you can ping Rally / Attack / Flag-here / SOS (P or middle mouse); pings
    appear on minimap/compass for your faction. Rally pings pull Defend/idle hippies;
    Attack pings bias Raid targets.
  - **SOS**: a hippie that takes damage auto-pings SOS; Defend hippies within 60 m
    respond.
  - **Retransmit: "TAKE A SHOT"** (60 s cooldown): every hippie on your mesh gets +35
    attention and wobbles for 2 s.
  - Enemy hippie status is hidden. A KO'd enemy hippie may drop its beacon (MOOP!).
    Picking it up taps that faction's mesh for 30 s: you see their hippies, their
    tasks and their planned Survey nodes. Acid Cop Vision does the same for all rivals.
- **Neutral hippies** wander the burn; recruit them with Flag Gifts. A captured camp's
  hippies become neutral.
- **Hoarding is villainy** (Canon: hoarding Flags caused the fall of Tartaria). Hearth
  stock above 24 makes your hippies' attention drain 50% faster ("the cold is the lack of
  activity").

## 9. Conquest

Hearth capture stages (per Hearth): **Safe → Threatened → Contained → Contested →
Overwritten → Captured**.

- **Threatened**: an enemy Survey facet or enemy Ley Line within 30 m.
- **Contained**: the Hearth's facet is inside an enemy Survey and not inside the
  owner's.
- **Contested**: the owner's vexillomancer is alive and **in person** within 8 m of the
  Hearth ("Hold the Hearth"). The owner's own Survey enclosing its Hearth does not slow
  pressure (every home ring encloses its Hearth from t = 0); the overlap still builds
  Crystal instability.
- Pressure per attacker 0→100. Base 6/s (~17 s bare). Modifiers (all in `CAPTURE`):
  - Contested ×0.6
  - each defending hippie within 12 m ×0.93 (floor 0.8)
  - Hearth Ward ×0.7
  - attacker Crystals within 45 m ×1.15 each
  - captured outpost (held by anyone but its founder) ×1.5
  - sudden death ×2, then +1 every 120 s after The Burn

  Pressure decays 3/s when not contained: a broken loop costs the attacker tempo
  (~33 s to drain), not the whole siege. With several attackers, the highest pressure
  leads the overwrite. These values come from 16-seed all-AI sweeps: a closed loop
  survives a median 4–8 s before defenders pull a wall Flag, so conversion must be quick.
- **Overwritten** at 100: a 3 s overwrite (cannot be stopped), then **Captured**:
  - the Hearth becomes the captor's outpost Hearth;
  - the loser's buildings become the captor's but disabled until repaired;
  - their planted Flags become neutral loose-able (pullable by anyone);
  - their hippies become neutral;
  - their GCC is destroyed.

  If the loser still holds another Hearth (an outpost), only what lies nearer the captured
  Hearth than any surviving one changes hands: its buildings, its planted Flags (neutral),
  its Signifiers (neutral, carried Flags dropped) and the GCC if parked there.

  A faction with no Hearth is eliminated. Last faction with a Hearth wins.
- **The Burn** (sudden death) at 14:00: the effigy burns with fireworks. Pressure ×2, +1
  every 120 s after, Phason Tides every 40 s, Omega Node Crystal pressure bonus doubled.
- **Dawn** at 30:00 (`DAWN_TIME`): if more than one camp still stands, the dominant one
  completes the Survey and wins (most Hearths, then largest Survey, then C.M.I.; no one
  else is eliminated). Requirements R4/R5 bound a match at 30 minutes and allow a
  "dominant" Hearth to win. In a 32-seed all-AI sweep ~10% of matches stall in a
  Hearth-trading duel past 30:00; Dawn ends only those.

Counterplay toolbox: pull a loop Flag (any one breaks the enclosure; the HUD shows the
loop's **critical Flags**), Phason Shift a loop node, plant your own Flag on their
implied node, wall off loop Flags, stand at your Hearth (Contested), Ward, Stabilize, Dialectics.

## 10. Command View (the GCC table)

- Tab toggles. The camera rises to a tilted top-down tactical view that looks like the
  GCC tabletop: parchment-and-ink map tint, lattice lines, faction Surveys, Flags as
  pins, red ley lines. WASD / edge pan, wheel zoom. The vexillomancer keeps fighting on
  its own (auto-swing at adjacent enemies). Time does not slow.
- Selection: drag-box hippies, click units/buildings/Flags. Right click issues a
  context order.
- Survey planning tools (plan = the faction's personal Survey Pattern, shown as ghost
  Flags everywhere, filled by Survey hippies):
  - **Node** — toggle individual nodes.
  - **Enclose** — click any point (e.g. an enemy Hearth); plan the cheapest loop around
    it. Node cost: 0 already held, 1 free, 3 a rival Flag (pull, then plant), impassable
    if blocked or Stabilize-protected. Rival Flags usually chain from their home ring to
    blocked ground, so a planner that refused to cross them would often find no loop at
    all. The prompt counts both ("21 new Flags · 5 rival Flags to pull") and explains a
    failure.
  - **Pentacle** — click a revealed focus; plan its 5 neighbours.
  - **Ring** — expand your home Survey by a radius step.
  - **Clear**.
- Camp Priorities sliders (job weights), build placement, DEGEN roster, pings.

## 11. Chakras and abilities (ritual economy)

Five **Flag chakras**, one per Ley direction (the five pentagrid families) and named for
the anatomy of a Flag. Align with Ritual at your Hearth: a 4 s channel, interruptible.
Costs: L1 30, L2 70, L3 130 Ritual. Ritual comes from drumming (0.1/s per drummer, four
per circle), Crystals (0.08/s each) and Saffron (0.3/s), never from territory size. In
all-AI play the first alignment lands at ~70–150 s and an L3 near minute 10.

## 13. NPC rivals

Same commands as the player. Layers:

1. **Director** (every 1.2 s): threat assessment (own Hearth stage, enemy loops closing,
   critical-Flag reach), opportunity (cheapest enemy enclosure via the loop planner,
   focus points), economy. Picks a posture per personality: EXPAND, ECONOMY,
   ATTACK(target), DEFEND, OPPORTUNIST.
2. **Survey planner**: maintains the plan using the shared planner functions
   (`planEnclosure`, `planPentacle`, home ring).
3. **Labour**: job weights plus squad orders (raid squads to critical Flags of enemy
   loops).
4. **Builder**: personality build order; places buildings inside own Survey; walls
   around exposed loop Flags and its Hearth under threat.
5. **Avatar pilot** (every tick): navigation, throwing Flags into the hardest planned
   nodes, pulling critical enemy Flags, standing at its own Hearth when contained,
   swinging at intruders, ability and drug use, restocking at the nearest own stocked
   Hearth (captured outposts become forward depots), and GCC errands (gifts, dialectics,
   simulacra). AI vexillomancers never push their GCC.

Difficulty (Chill / Normal / Hard / Vexillosaint) scales reaction delay, decision
interval, throw accuracy, and ability usage. NPCs never cheat on information beyond what
their faction can see.

## 14. Presentation

- Golden-hour start, sunset ~6:20, night from ~10:00 (flags and ley lines glow, fairy
  lights on domes), fireworks at The Burn (14:00), deepest night ~20:00, blue hour
  ~26–28 min, sunrise exactly at Dawn (30:00). Night never clips to white: Survey glass
  is a tint, bloom keeps only energy above its threshold.
- Yellow Flags with a waving cloth shader are always the brightest saturated objects.
  Ley lines are glowing beams in faction colour. Survey facets are translucent crystal
  glass tinted by faction, with a sweeping fill animation when a loop closes. Crystals
  are tall translucent pink/blue prisms with light pillars (2017 game homage). Survey
  View tints the ground with the 2017 cyan/red noise field and fine white lattice lines.
- Hearth stage is always visible: HUD rail with all four Hearths, a world-space ring
  around each Hearth, and an SOS pulse on the minimap.
- HUD: Flags carried/stock in an ornate frame ("20 FLAGS"), lumber, ritual, hippies,
  attention, clock, next tide and Burn, ability bar (1–5), drug slots (6–8), build keys,
  C.M.I. counter (ornate, bottom right), DEGEN minimap (pentagonal frame), event feed,
  Hearth rail.
- Title screen: orbiting cinematic around a GCC with Flags and a manifesting Crystal,
  lore quote rotation.
- Audio: procedural WebAudio only (no asset files). Festival bass, drum circle
  polyrhythm, wind and flag flap, pentatonic plant chimes, ley-line hum chords, crystal
  choir, phason glass-glitch, capture alarm, UI blips. Intensity follows threat.

```
    lattice/  pentagrid generation, Lattice class, flips, survey geometry, planners
    physics/  2.5D collision world (avatars, thrown flags, pieces, buildings, obstacles)
    nav/      nav grid + A* + LOS for hippies
    map/      burn map generator
    systems/  per-rule systems called by simulation.ts in fixed order
  ai/         NPC director + avatar pilot (reads World, emits Commands)
  render/     three.js presentation (reads World + Session, consumes GameEvents)
  game/       app bootstrap, fixed-step loop, input, camera rig, player controller,
              command view, build mode, session state
  ui/         DOM HUD and screens (reads World + Session, dispatches Commands)
  audio/      procedural WebAudio (consumes GameEvents + World)
```

Rules:

- Sim truth lives only in `World`. Everything else is a view or a command producer.
  Player and AI submit the same `Command` objects through `world.submit()`.
- Fixed 60 Hz simulation step (`SIM_DT`); render each animation frame from latest state.
- Seeded RNG (`world.rng`) for all sim randomness. No `Math.random` in `sim/` or `ai/`.
- Events: systems call `world.emit(event)`; consumers read `world.drainEvents()` once per
  frame (the app fans them out to render/ui/audio).
- Performance targets: 60 fps at 1080p on a mid laptop GPU; sim step < 4 ms with ~150
  hippies. Instanced/batched meshes for hippies, flags, pieces, props and lattice;
  map-wide props are split into 8×8 culling tiles; no material is shared between
  instanced and non-instanced draws (it forces program re-derivation every frame).
- Evals (`web/bench/`): `bun bench/sim-eval.ts` (`npm run eval:sim`; p95 tick ms for an
  all-AI match, per-system breakdown, `--top`, `--probes`), `bun bench/nav-eval.ts`
  (replays a recorded nav workload), and `bench/render-eval.js` (browser: p95 frame ms,
  JS split, draw calls, triangles, program-variant switches). Each yields a single score,
  so they plug into the harness `/ratchet` hill-climb. Measure on a hardware-GPU Chrome
  (`--use-angle=vulkan`); the shared headless browser is SwiftShader.
- Tests: vitest for lattice/survey/capture/AI-planner invariants and a headless full
  match (4 AI factions) that must reach a winner.
