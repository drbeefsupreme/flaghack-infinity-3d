## Format and modifications

The files are the publicly downloadable high-quality MP3 previews provided by Freesound,
not the login-gated original WAV downloads. The distributed MP3 bytes are unchanged.
During playback the game peak-normalizes decoded audio to 0.85, adjusts gain, adds shared
reverb, and applies the existing boss compressor and output limiter. No pitch/time changes.
The sound effects remain subject to player master/SFX volume and mute controls.

All other boss effects (death, footsteps, charge, beam, explosions and rubble) are
original procedural WebAudio synthesis in this repository.
